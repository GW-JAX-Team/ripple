Validity Ranges¤

ripple ports the LAL implementations but does not enforce their parameter checks. Outside these ranges the models extrapolate silently. Each model links to its LAL 7.26 documentation; limits that page does not state are checks in the LALSuite 7.26.16 source.

Compact-binary coalescences¤

The table lists what LAL rejects and the calibrated range stated in LAL's reviewed model documentation, with \(q = m_1/m_2 \ge 1\). LAL documents every model as usable outside its calibrated range, with unverified accuracy there.

Waveform LAL rejects Calibrated range
TaylorF2 — Post-Newtonian; no calibration
IMRPhenomD \(\lvert\chi_i\rvert > 1\) \(q = 1, 4, 8, 18\) with spins in \([-0.95, 0.98]\), \([-0.75, 0.75]\), \([-0.85, 0.85]\), \([-0.8, 0.4]\) respectively
IMRPhenomHM \(\lvert\chi_i\rvert > 1\) As IMRPhenomD; the higher modes are not calibrated to numerical relativity
IMRPhenomPv2 \(\lvert\boldsymbol{\chi}_i\rvert > 1\) As IMRPhenomD; LAL also warns for strongly anti-aligned spins, where the precession opening angle is no longer tracked
IMRPhenomD_NRTidalv2 \(\lvert\chi_i\rvert > 1\), \(\Lambda_i < 0\) As IMRPhenomD; tides to \(q = 1.5\) and \(\kappa_2^T \in [40, 5000]\)
IMRPhenomPv2_NRTidalv2 \(\lvert\boldsymbol{\chi}_i\rvert > 1\), \(\Lambda_i < 0\) As IMRPhenomD_NRTidalv2; the review found unphysical features for \(q < 1.5\) with both \(\Lambda_i > 2000\)
IMRPhenomNSBH \(m_\mathrm{NS} > 3\,M_\odot\), \(q \notin [1, 100]\), \(\Lambda_\mathrm{NS} \notin [0, 5000]\); a negative inspiral frequency derivative Amplitude at \(q = 2, 3\) with BH spin in \([-0.5, 0.75]\) and \(q = 4, 5\) with BH spin in \([0, 0.75]\); non-spinning NS with \(m_\mathrm{NS} \ge 1\,M_\odot\); phase as IMRPhenomD_NRTidalv2
IMRPhenomXAS, IMRPhenomXHM \(q > 1000\), \(\lvert\chi_i\rvert > 1\) \(q\) from 1 to 1000; LAL warns beyond the numerical-relativity data (\(q > 20\)) and for \(\lvert\chi_i\rvert > 0.99\)
IMRPhenomXP, IMRPhenomXPHM \(q > 1000\), \(\lvert\boldsymbol{\chi}_i\rvert > 1\) As IMRPhenomXAS
IMRPhenomXAS_NRTidalv3, IMRPhenomXP_NRTidalv3 As the BBH model, and \(\Lambda_i < 0\) As the BBH model; tides to \(q = 2\) and \(\kappa_2^T \in [40, 5000]\)

LAL applies the IMRPhenomNSBH limit \(m_\mathrm{NS} \le 3\,M_\odot\) to the detector-frame mass, so it also rejects ordinary systems at redshift \(z \gtrsim 1.15\); ripple's model has no such limit.

Bursts¤

LAL rejects a negative Q, f_0 or hrss for SineGaussian, and an eccentricity e outside \([0, 1]\).

Continuous waves¤

  • All sample times must lie within the span of the ephemeris files. LAL rejects other times; ripple clamps to the ends of the tables and returns unphysical values.
  • LAL rejects a right ascension outside \([-2\pi, 2\pi]\) or a declination outside \([-\pi/2, \pi/2]\).
  • BinaryPulsarSignal models elliptical orbits only, \(0 \le\) ecc \(< 1\). LAL rejects a negative eccentricity; ripple returns non-finite values for ecc \(\ge 1\).
